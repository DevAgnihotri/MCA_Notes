# 🔹 1. EVOLUTION OF SOFTWARE QUALITY ASSURANCE (SQA)

---

### 📌 Definition:

> **Software Quality Assurance (SQA) is a systematic process to ensure that software meets specified quality standards.**

---

### 📖 Explanation (6–7 lines):

SQA ka concept time ke saath evolve hua hai. Initially focus sirf testing pe tha, lekin ab pura lifecycle cover karta hai. Pehle “after development checking” hota tha, ab “before + during + after development” sab cover hota hai. Modern SQA preventive approach follow karta hai, jisme defects ko pehle hi avoid kiya jata hai. Yeh process-oriented ho gaya hai instead of product-only focus. Agile aur DevOps ke saath SQA aur integrated ho gaya hai.

💡 Hinglish:
“Pehle bug pakadte the… ab bug hone hi nahi dete 😄”

---

👉 Mnemonic: **“PAST → PRO”**
(P → Product check, A → After development, S → Simple testing, T → Traditional)
→
(P → Process focus, R → Risk prevention, O → Overall lifecycle)

# 🔹 2. QUALITY ENGINEERING vs QUALITY ASSURANCE ⭐

(**Question Covered: Is Quality Engineering different from QA? – Section A Q1f**)

---

### 📌 Definitions:

* **Quality Assurance (QA):**

> Process-oriented approach to prevent defects

* **Quality Engineering (QE):**

> Advanced approach focusing on automation, continuous improvement, and testing integration

---

### 📖 Explanation (6–7 lines):

QA traditional approach hai jo processes define karta hai to ensure quality. QE ek modern concept hai jo automation, CI/CD aur tools use karta hai for continuous quality. QA ka focus defect prevention pe hota hai, jabki QE ka focus **end-to-end quality engineering** pe hota hai including testing, monitoring, and feedback. QE zyada proactive aur technology-driven hota hai.

---

👉 Mnemonic: **“OLD vs SMART”**

---

## ⚖️ Difference Table

| Feature  | QA            | QE                       |
| -------- | ------------- | ------------------------ |
| Approach | Process-based | Engineering-based        |
| Focus    | Prevention    | Automation + Improvement |
| Tools    | Limited       | Advanced tools           |
| Nature   | Reactive      | Proactive                |

---

💡 Hinglish:
“QA bolta hai process follow karo… QE bolta hai system automate karo 😄”

# 🔹 9. SIX SIGMA ⭐ (VERY IMPORTANT – SECTION C)

(**Question Covered: Explain Six Sigma with example – Section C Q6b**)

---

### 📌 Definition:

> **Six Sigma is a data-driven methodology to reduce defects and improve quality.**

---

### 📖 Explanation (7–8 lines – HEAVY DETAIL):

Six Sigma ka aim hota hai defects ko minimum karna (almost zero). Yeh statistical approach use karta hai process improvement ke liye. Iska goal hota hai **3.4 defects per million opportunities**. Yeh DMAIC model follow karta hai. Companies iska use karti hain efficiency aur quality improve karne ke liye.

---

👉 Mnemonic: **“DMAIC”**

---

## 🔸 Phases:

1. **Define** → Problem identify
2. **Measure** → Data collect
3. **Analyze** → Root cause
4. **Improve** → Fix issues
5. **Control** → Maintain quality

---

## 🔸 Example ⭐

👉 Suppose ek software company mein 1000 bugs aa rahe hain:

* Measure → bugs count
* Analyze → major module identify
* Improve → fix coding practices
* Result → bugs reduce drastically

---

💡 Hinglish:
“Data dekh ke system sudharo 😤”

---

## 🔸 Advantages:

* High accuracy
* Data-driven

## 🔸 Disadvantages:

* Complex
* Requires expertise
